وَلِلَّهِ الْأَسْمَاءُ الْحُسْنَىٰ فَادْعُوهُ بِهَا

“To Allah belong the most beautiful names, so call upon Him by them.” (al-A'raf 7:180)

The Prophet ﷺ said: “Allah has ninety-nine names, one hundred less one. Whoever preserves them will enter Paradise.” (al-Bukhari, Muslim)

About the benefits

The benefits of recitation listed in this app come from traditional devotional literature passed down by scholars and teachers. Except where a hadith is cited, they are not established by authentic hadith. Recite with sincerity, and ask a qualified scholar for guidance.

About the classifications

The thematic categories group names by meaning to help with reflection. The Jalal (majesty), Jamal (beauty) and Kamal (perfection) grouping follows a traditional approach; scholars differ on where some names belong.
